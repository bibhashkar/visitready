# Devpost Submission Template — VisitReady

## Project Name
VisitReady

## Tagline
Know what to ask before you walk in. Understand what came back.

## The problem it solves
Most health AI tools decode lab results after the fact — but by then you've
already waited 2–4 weeks for your appointment without knowing what to request.

VisitReady fixes the step nobody addresses: the moment before your appointment.

Two problems solved:
1. "I have an appointment — what tests should I actually ask for?"
2. "Results came back — what does any of this mean?"

## Innovation approach
VisitReady is the only tool that works in both directions:

**Pre-visit (unique):** Answer 10 questions about your age, symptoms, lifestyle,
and family history. Get a personalized blood test panel ranked by priority
(essential / recommended / optional) — with specific reasons tied to YOUR
profile, not generic advice. Plus doctor questions ready to screenshot.

**Results decoder (context-aware):** Paste your lab report. Get plain-English
explanations for every marker, an interactive body map showing which organ
systems are flagged, and follow-up questions for your doctor.

**What makes it different:** If you used the pre-visit prep first, the results
decoder uses your profile for personalized interpretation — connecting your
symptoms to your findings, adjusting risk language for your family history,
and flagging if important tests were never ordered.
